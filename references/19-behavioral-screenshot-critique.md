# Helix Behavioral Screenshot Critique

Purpose: critique screenshots for behavior change, not just visual aesthetics.

## Required questions per screenshot
1. What does the user understand in 5 seconds?
2. What current anxiety is named or reduced?
3. What belief should change after this screen?
4. What action is obvious?
5. What makes the action feel safe/easy?
6. What feels like effort, risk, or confusion?
7. What visual element is doing the behavioral work?
8. What reference pattern is being used or missing?
9. What is the most important repair?

## Score dimensions
- recognition: user sees themselves/problem immediately
- relief: the UI lowers anxiety rather than amplifying it
- control: user understands options and reversibility
- clarity: product object explains the product without a tour
- trust: data/cost/risk objections addressed honestly
- action: CTA is timely, specific, and low friction

## Output format
Use `schemas/behavioral-critique.schema.json`.

Every critique must include:
- screenshot path
- viewport
- behavioral read
- visible blockers
- repair priorities
- expected impact
